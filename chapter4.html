Chapter 4: The Calculator in Your Computer

1. Basic Math

Python is the world's most powerful calculator! You can use addition (+), subtraction (-), multiplication (*), and division (/).

Example A: Addition

print(5 + 5)

Example B: Subtraction

print(10 - 2)
Assignment 1: Age in Days

2. Remainders and Whole Numbers

Sometimes you can't divide things evenly. The modulo operator (%) tells you what is left over. The floor division operator (//) tells you how many whole times it fits without the decimals.

Assignment 2: Pizza Slice Divider

3. Exponents (Powers)

Want to multiply a number by itself many times? Use double stars (**). For example, 2 ** 3 is 2 times 2 times 2 (which equals 8).

Assignment 3: Area of a Room

4. Converting Text to Numbers

Remember that input() always gives you text (a String). If you want to do math with a user's answer, you have to turn it into an integer (a whole number) using int()!

5. Doing Math with Variables

You can use variables in math equations just like regular numbers. You can even use a variable to update itself!
