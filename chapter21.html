Chapter 21: AI & LLMs (Groq, Prompts & RAG) 🚀

1. How Large Language Models Work (Tokens & Probability)

An LLM (Large Language Model) like Llama 3 or GPT-4 is a massive neural network trained on trillions of words. At its core, an LLM works by taking input text, breaking it into Tokens (words or word parts), and calculating the mathematical probability of the very next token!

Input: "The wizard cast a powerful..." → [Next Token Probabilities: "spell": 85%, "glow": 10%, "pizza": 0.01%]

2. Temperature & Message Roles (`system`, `user`, `assistant`)

When communicating with modern AI APIs, we structure our request as a list of role-based messages:

  • system: Defines the AI's persona, rules, and boundaries (e.g. "You are an expert Python tutor who speaks like a pirate").
  • user: The human prompt or question.
  • assistant: Previous responses from the AI.
  • Temperature: Controls randomness. 0.0 = deterministic & precise (ideal for math/coding), while 0.8+ = creative & inventive (ideal for storytelling).

3. Prompt Engineering (Few-Shot & Chain-of-Thought)

How you phrase your prompt radically affects answer quality! Two key industry techniques:

  1. Few-Shot Prompting: Providing 2-3 examples of input and expected output before asking your question.
  2. Chain-of-Thought (CoT): Instructing the AI to "Think step-by-step" before producing the final answer.
